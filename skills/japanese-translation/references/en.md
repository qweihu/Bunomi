# English (en)

- Use natural English; for lyrics prefer singable, idiomatic phrasing when the
  caller requests it, otherwise faithful prose.
- Keep one English line per Japanese line and preserve blank-line grouping.
- Do not add ruby to English lines; the Japanese lines keep their ruby.
- Use Hepburn romanization for Japanese proper nouns without an established
  English name; keep quoted or intentional Japanese terms as-is.
- Kana in an English line usually means an untranslated fragment; fix it unless
  it is a quoted term, then say so in the conversation.
- Do not add footnotes, explanations, or commentary.
